## What went well

- Locking the scope to a single location (the lighthouse) early on
- Reusing one lighting rig for almost every "scare" beat
- Getting a second pair of eyes on the sound design early

## What I'd change

- Start playtesting earlier - the pacing of the second act only clicked in the final week
- Write the ending first, then work backwards
